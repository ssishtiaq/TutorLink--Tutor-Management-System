About TutorLink

TutorLink is a web-based tutoring management platform. It helps students discover tutors, request learning sessions, and keep track of their bookings. Tutors can share their subjects and availability, while managers monitor tutoring activities and administrators manage the platform.

Our Services

  • Find Tutors: Browse tutor profiles and subjects.
  • Session Booking: Request a tutoring session at a suitable time.
  • Booking Tracking: Check whether a session is pending, confirmed, or completed.
  • Tutor Profiles: Review tutor information and teaching subjects.
  • Feedback: Share feedback after a tutoring session.
  • Management: Support platform administration and monitoring.

How It Works

  1. Create an account or log in.
  2. Students browse tutor profiles and choose a suitable tutor.
  3. Students submit a session booking request.
  4. Tutors review requests and manage their sessions.
  5. Students track bookings and leave feedback after a session.
